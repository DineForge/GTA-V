    Full browser & troubleshooting guide ↗
    01 What do I need to play?

    A current Chrome or Edge browser with hardware acceleration, WebGPU, BC texture compression, shared memory, and WebAssembly Memory64. Use a capable desktop GPU and plenty of available RAM. Device checks confirm basic features, not guaranteed gameplay.

    02 How do the controls work?

    Use keyboard and mouse. Click the game to capture your mouse; press Esc to release it. Standard gamepads have experimental input mapping. Press F8 to hide or restore the extra tools. Touch support depends on your device meeting the engine requirements.

    03 Loading stopped. What now?

    Give the first load time to download assets and compile shaders. If it stops, enable graphics acceleration, update your browser and graphics driver, and try the 720p / 30 FPS profile. Open the browser guide for diagnostics. Black-world rendering and GPU device loss are known issues in this snapshot.

    04 What about saves and multiplayer?

    Story saves belong to this browser and site. Keep your browser data; saves from other websites do not transfer automatically. This build is single-player. GTA Online and shared-world multiplayer are not included.

    05 Where does the game run?

    The compiled browser game runs on your device at this site's address. Engine and game data are stored in this site's Netlify Blobs storage and served from this site's address. An internet connection is required. This is an experimental compiled snapshot, not the original engine source.
